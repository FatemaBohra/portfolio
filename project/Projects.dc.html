Zinema — Movie Booking App

Full-Stack Developer · Personal Project

A movie booking platform built independently to master cloud-native development and third-party integrations. The platform includes Auth0 authentication with role-based access, an admin panel for managing movies and showtimes, and a booking history page with cancellation option.
